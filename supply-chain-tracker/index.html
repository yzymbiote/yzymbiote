No sponsor? You may not need one

Three ways non-locals get the right to work in Hong Kong
IANGYou study at a Hong Kong university

Non-local graduates of full-time degree programmes in Hong Kong can stay and work after graduating without an employer sponsoring them. While you study, you can also do summer internships.

TTPS · Category CYou graduated from a top-ranked university

The Top Talent Pass Scheme gives graduates of listed top universities (within the last 5 years) a visa to look for work in Hong Kong. You don't need a job offer first.

Working HolidayYou're 18–30 from a partner country

Hong Kong has working holiday agreements with countries including the UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Japan and Korea. It suits a summer internship or a first year of work.

Roles marked Right to work needed are usually open to you if you hold one of these. Rules and quotas change, so check the current terms at immd.gov.hk.
